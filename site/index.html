    About this data

    Every figure comes from the county's yearly discretionary spending reports. Each report line is kept, and every closing balance the reports print matches the opening balance minus the payments.

    Some commissioners also hold older balances in a general fund account and a 2015 bond fund. Those balances are included here.

    The reports do not label spending by type. Each line was sorted into gifts, travel and office, or purchases for others by reading its description.

    County policy requires a commission resolution for every gift. Each gift was matched to its resolution in the county's resolution archive by district, amount, name and date, and the FY2023 and FY2024 matches agree with the county's 2025 audit. "No match found" means this search did not find one, not that none exists. "Said in committee" comes from the meeting minutes.

    A gift's project comes from the spending report. The county's 2025 audit found that gifts were "not completely verified" as used for their intended purpose. Outcomes here are blank except where that audit reported one.
