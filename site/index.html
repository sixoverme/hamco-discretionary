    About this data

    Every figure comes from the county's yearly discretionary spending reports. Each report line is kept, and every closing balance the reports print matches the opening balance minus the payments.

    Some commissioners also hold older balances in a general fund account and a 2015 bond fund. Those balances are included here.

    The reports do not label spending by type. For past years, each line was sorted into gifts, travel and office, or purchases for others by reading its description, so a few lines may be sorted wrong. The county's account codes can replace this sorting.

    Resolution numbers for FY2023 and FY2024 gifts come from the county's 2025 audit. Other years' resolution numbers, and every gift's goal and outcome, have not been published.
